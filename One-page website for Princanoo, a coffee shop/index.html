Our Story

Small shop, big love for coffee

Princanoo started as a simple idea: serve honest, well-made coffee in a place that feels like home. Every cup is brewed fresh, every guest is welcomed like a neighbor.

From classic espresso to creamy Spanish lattes and fresh pastries, our menu is short, focused, and made with quality ingredients.
